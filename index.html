Our Process

Every vehicle treated with a proven, meticulous 4-step system.

01

Decontamination

Full wash, foam pre-soak, tar remover, Iron-X fallout removal and clay bar treatment to strip every contaminant.

02

Paint Correction

Multi-stage machine polishing with 3M Perfect-It compounds and precision cutting pads to eliminate swirls and scratches.

03

Ceramic Coating

Application of nano-ceramic or graphene coating for a glass-hard, hydrophobic shield that lasts years.

04

Final Inspection

Thorough inspection under specialist lighting, interior dressing and a full quality-control check before handover.
